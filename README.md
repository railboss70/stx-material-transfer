# STX Material Transfer

Phone-first inventory transfer and bill of lading for STX Railroad Construction.

Search the 2025 material catalog, auto-fill item codes, save drafts on the phone, and send the PDF to **materials@stxrailroad.com**.

## Open on your iPhone

Same flow as Daily Summary and PrecisionTrack:

1. Open this link in **Safari** (not Chrome):
   https://railboss70.github.io/stx-material-transfer/
2. Tap **Share**.
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Name it **STX Transfer** and Add.

Transfers stay on the phone until you send them. Form data is not uploaded to GitHub.

## Office email

Default recipient: `materials@stxrailroad.com`
